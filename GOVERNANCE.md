# Governance

## Principles

The collection is published collectively as the **Open Geomatics Education Group**. No single member owns it.
Each resource credits its individual authors, contributors and reviewers.

## Roles

| Role | Who | Rights and duties |
|---|---|---|
| **Maintainers** | At least 3 members, ideally from different institutions | Owners of the GitHub Organization and curators of the Zenodo Community. Merge pull requests, manage releases, admit members. |
| **Members** | Teachers and researchers who contribute regularly | Write access to the repository; review others' contributions. |
| **Contributors** | Anyone | Propose changes via issues and pull requests. |

Current maintainers are listed on the [About & Credits](about.qmd) page.

## Decisions

- **Content contributions:** published after approval by at least **one member other than the author**.
- **Structural changes** (licences, name, governance, platforms): require agreement of a **majority of maintainers**, discussed in a public issue open for at least 14 days.
- Disagreements are discussed openly in issues; maintainers decide by majority if consensus is not reached.

## Membership

- Anyone with at least one published resource or two reviewed contributions may ask to become a member.
- Maintainers are proposed by any member and confirmed by majority of existing maintainers.
- Members inactive for more than 2 years may be moved to "emeritus"; their credits remain unchanged.

## Continuity

- The GitHub Organization always has **at least 2 owners** from different institutions.
- Zenodo records are created by the group custodian account or shared with all maintainers ("manage" permission).
- Credentials of shared service accounts (e.g. the Zenodo custodian) are stored in a password manager shared by maintainers and rotated when a maintainer leaves.
- If a maintainer leaves, their author credits remain; administrative rights are transferred within 30 days.

## Releases

- A **collection release** (new DOI version) is published at least once per academic year.
- Single resources receive their own DOI when declared mature by their authors and one reviewer.
